Never miss a service again. Machine Maintenance Tracker keeps the maintenance of all your vehicles and machines in one place: motorcycles, cars, generators and any other equipment that needs regular care.

100% PRIVATE
All your data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. It works offline, always. You can export and import your data whenever you want.

FEATURES
• Track everything: motorcycles, cars, generators, industrial machines. Add photos, specifications and custom details for each one
• Smart reminders: set maintenance intervals by time (days) or by usage (kilometers or hours) and get notified when service is due
• Visual status: color-coded indicators show at a glance what is optimal, approaching or overdue
• Complete records: log oil changes, filter replacements, inspections and fuel fills, with costs, notes and odometer readings
• Multiple machines: manage your whole garage in a single app
• Beautiful dark theme with a clean interface

MAINTENANCE TYPES
Oil changes, filter service, tire maintenance, brake system, coolant, spark plugs, chain/belt service, general service, fuel logging and custom types.

Keep your machines reliable and extend their lifespan, without spreadsheets or paper notebooks.
